Who We Are & Leadership

Hi, I'm Stefan — I'm 21, from Brazil, and I founded Freedom Technology. I fell in love with immersive audio games and realized that technology should be free and accessible for anyone who is blind or has low vision.

Freedom Technology is currently a one-person project with a big heart and an even bigger mission: making technology accessible to everyone — whether you use Windows, Linux, Android, or iOS with a screen reader.

Our goal is to build programs and tools that work for people who are blind, in their everyday lives on computers and devices. Because nobody should be left behind in the digital world.

Technological Freedom & Social Inclusion

Technological freedom means having the power to use any digital tool without barriers. For people who are blind, easy access to software, games, and operating systems is essential — for work, learning, and fun.

Social inclusion becomes real when everyone has the same opportunities and choices in the digital world. That's what drives us every single day.

Team Growth & Contributions

We're growing and looking for contributors! If you have programming skills — whether you code by hand or use AI-assisted tools — we'd love to have you on board. Open source collaboration is at the heart of everything we do.
