About Freedom Technology

Our story, leadership, core principles, and direct contact.

Who We Are & Leadership

My name is Stefan. I'm from Brazil, 21 years old, and the founding leader of Freedom Technology. I developed an immense passion for immersive audio games and technological freedom for any blind or visually impaired person.

Freedom Technology is currently a one-person team driven by a single mission: technological freedom and accessibility for all blind individuals — whether they use Windows, Linux, Android, or iOS with screen readers.

Our goal is to develop programs and tools that are accessible to blind people for everyday use on computers and related devices. No one should be left behind in the digital world.

Technological Freedom & Social Inclusion

Technological freedom means having the autonomy to use any digital tool without barriers. For a blind person, effortless access to software, games, and operating systems is essential for citizenship, education, and leisure.

Social inclusion truly happens when blind users have the same opportunities and power of choice in the digital world. That is what we build for — every day.

Team Growth & Contributions

The team hopes to grow. Anyone with strong programming skills — whether using artificial intelligence or manual coding — who wishes to contribute is very welcome! Open source collaboration is at the heart of what we do.
