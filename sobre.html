About Us

Who We Are

Hi, I'm Stefan — 21, from Brazil, founder of Freedom Technology. I'm passionate about immersive audio games and making technology accessible to anyone who is blind or has low vision.

Freedom Technology is currently a one-person project with a big mission: accessible tools for people who use screen readers on Windows, Linux, Android, or iOS.

Our Mission

Technological freedom means having the power to use any digital tool without barriers. Easy access to software, games, and systems is essential for work, learning, and fun.

Inclusion becomes real when everyone has the same opportunities in the digital world. That's what drives us every day.

Contributors Welcome

We're looking for contributors. If you have programming skills — whether you code by hand or use AI-assisted tools — we'd love to have you. Open source is at the heart of what we do.
